````md
# Business Growth
# Package Definition

**Package:** Business Growth  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Agency Template Standard  
**Package Type:** Growth-Oriented Business Website  
**Positioning:** Marketing + Content + Lead Generation

---

# 1. Purpose

The Business Growth package is the next level above Starter Presence.

It is designed for businesses whose website needs to do more than establish credibility and provide basic company information.

The Business Growth website is intended to actively support:

- Product and service discovery
- Lead generation
- Quote requests
- Marketing campaigns
- Content publishing
- Project and portfolio presentation
- Customer trust building
- Ongoing business communication

The central purpose is:

> **Turn the website from a company profile into an active business-growth and lead-generation platform.**

---

# 2. Package Positioning

Business Growth must create a clear value difference from Starter Presence.

## Starter Presence

Primary purpose:

> Establish a professional online presence.

Typical outcome:

```text
Visitor
    ↓
Understand Company
    ↓
View Services / Projects
    ↓
Contact Company
````

## Business Growth

Primary purpose:

> Use the website to actively market the business, present a larger body of information, and generate qualified inquiries.

Typical outcome:

```text
Visitor
    ↓
Discover Business
    ↓
Explore Services / Products
    ↓
Compare / Evaluate
    ↓
View Projects / Proof / Testimonials
    ↓
Read Content / Updates
    ↓
Request Quote / Make Inquiry
    ↓
Lead Generated
```

Business Growth must therefore introduce meaningful functionality and content architecture that does not exist in the basic package.

# 5. Business Problems This Package Solves

Business Growth is appropriate when a client has one or more of the following problems:

### Problem 1 — Too much information for a basic website

The client has many:

* Services
* Products
* Projects
* Locations
* Teams
* Certifications
* Resources

### Problem 2 — Customers need more detail

Visitors need to:

* Compare services
* Explore product categories
* Review specifications
* Read project information
* Download brochures
* Understand the company's capabilities

### Problem 3 — The company needs more inquiries

The website needs:

* Stronger CTAs
* Quote requests
* Inquiry forms
* Service-specific inquiries
* Product inquiries
* WhatsApp
* Contact pathways

### Problem 4 — The website needs to support marketing

The company wants to publish:

* News
* Articles
* Updates
* Projects
* Success stories
* Educational content

### Problem 5 — The company needs measurable results

The business wants to understand:

* Where visitors go
* What they interact with
* Which CTAs perform
* Which pages generate interest
* Which forms produce leads

# 7. Website Capacity

The package supports up to **10 core page templates/areas**, tailored to the client's requirements.

The agency must distinguish between:

### Core Page Templates

Reusable page structures such as:

```text
Home
About
Services
Service Detail
Products
Product Detail
Projects
Project Detail
Blog
Blog Article
Contact
```

and:

### Content Entries

Individual records generated from those templates.

Example:

```text
Service Detail Template
    ├── Service A
    ├── Service B
    ├── Service C
    └── Service D
```

Therefore:

> A client having 20 products does not automatically mean the website has 20 separately designed "core pages."

Those products may use one approved Product Detail template.

This distinction is fundamental to the architecture of the package.

# 16. Request for Quote (RFQ)

RFQ is a major Business Growth capability.

```text

Company
Email
Phone

Quantity

```

Only collect fields that are actually useful for the client's sales process.

Do not create unnecessarily long RFQ forms.

---

# 17. Lead Quality Principle

The purpose of an RFQ is not to collect the maximum amount of data.

The purpose is:

> **Collect enough information to help the business understand and respond to a qualified inquiry.**

Every field should have a reason.

# 27. Search

Internal search is **not automatically included**.

It may be considered when the client's content volume genuinely requires it.

Examples:

* Large product catalog
* Large service library
* Large article library
* Large project portfolio

For a small number of content entries, simple category navigation is preferable.

# 31. What Business Growth Does NOT Include

The package remains a marketing/content website.

It does not automatically include:

* E-commerce
* Online payments
* Customer accounts
* Inventory systems
* Booking engines
* ERP integrations
* CRM integrations
* Membership systems
* Mobile applications
* AI chatbots
* Custom dashboards
* Client CMS/admin panel

These are separate products, add-ons, or custom projects.

# 32. CMS Boundary

Business Growth does not automatically include a client-facing CMS.

By default:

> **The agency manages structured content updates through the maintenance service.**

A client CMS/admin panel may be added as a separate capability if there is a strong business requirement.

This prevents unnecessary application complexity.

# 39. Template-First Principle

Business Growth is an **agency template system**.

The agency should not create a separate technical architecture from scratch for every client.

Instead:

```text
Business Growth Template
        ↓
Approved Client Requirements
        ↓
Select Required Modules
        ↓
Apply Client Branding
        ↓
Apply Approved UI
        ↓
Populate Client Content
        ↓
Configure Integrations
        ↓
Test
        ↓
Deploy
```

This makes delivery faster and more consistent.

# 42. Design Reference Rule

Business Growth must follow the same fundamental agency rule established for Starter Presence:

> **The approved client/project UI is the visual source of truth.**

For every client project:

```text
Client UI Reference
       ↓
Business Growth Design System
       ↓
Page Template
       ↓
Component System
       ↓
Implementation
```

The AI coding agent must implement the provided design.

It must not:

* Invent its own design.
* Replace the approved layout.
* Add generic SaaS patterns.
* Redesign components.
* Change the visual hierarchy without approval.

# 44. Upgrade Principle

A client should have a clear reason to upgrade from Starter Presence to Business Growth.

Upgrade when the business needs:

```text
More content
        +
More structured information
        +
More detailed pages
        +
More lead-generation tools
        +
Marketing content
        +
```
