````md
# 1. Purpose

This document defines how client content, media, documents, downloads, brand assets, and related website resources are collected, organized, validated, processed, stored, referenced, published, updated, and retired within the Business Growth package.

Business Growth supports significantly more content than Starter Presence.

The website may contain:

- Products
- Product categories
- Product specifications
- Services
- Service categories
- Projects
- Case studies
- Testimonials
- Team members
- Locations
- Certifications
- Partners
- Blog/news
- Resources
- Brochures
- Catalogs
- Technical documents
- Images
- Videos
- Property listings
- Vehicles
- Accommodation
- Tours
- Programs
- Healthcare services
- Other sector-specific content

The objective is to keep all content:

- Organized
- Accurate
- Approved
- Reusable
- Search-friendly
- Secure
- Optimized
- Traceable
- Maintainable
````

# 3. Content Management Principle

The agency should separate:

```text
Content
   ↓
Assets
   ↓
Presentation
```

For example:

```text
Product Data
   +
Product Images
   +
Product Brochure
   ↓
Product Detail Template
   ↓
Approved Client UI
```

The same product data and assets may be reused in:

* Product listing
* Product detail
* Homepage
* Related content
* Search
* RFQ context
* SEO metadata

---

# 4. Content Is a Business Asset

Client content should be treated as a business asset, not temporary filler.

Examples:

* Company history
* Product information
* Technical specifications
* Project records
* Testimonials
* Certifications
* Brochures
* Photography
* Videos

Content should therefore be:

```text
Collected
→ Organized
→ Verified
→ Approved
→ Structured
→ Published
→ Maintained
→ Archived
```

---

# 5. Content Sources

Content may come from:

```text
Client
Client's existing website
Official company documents
Approved marketing materials
Existing databases
Official social media
Agency-assisted editing
Approved external sources
```

The source of important factual information should be known.

---

# 6. Content Source Priority

When multiple versions of the same information exist:

```text
Current Client-Approved Information
        ↓
Current Official Company Material
        ↓
Earlier Approved Material
        ↓
Other Reference Material
```

Do not automatically use the oldest or easiest-to-find information.

# 10. Content Ownership

The client remains responsible for the factual accuracy of information they provide.

The agency is responsible for:

* Organization
* Presentation
* Formatting
* Technical implementation
* Content model mapping
* Asset optimization

The agency should not knowingly publish inaccurate information.

# 11. Content Approval

Content must follow an approval process:

```text
Draft
 ↓
Agency Review
 ↓
Client Review
 ↓
Corrections
 ↓
Client Approval
 ↓
Production
```

Important factual content should not be treated as approved merely because it was received.

---

# 12. Content Approval Record

For important content batches, record:

```text
Content:
Source:
Prepared By:
Reviewed By:
Client Approved:
Approval Date:
Notes:
```

---

# 13. Content Freeze

Before final QA, establish a content freeze.

```text
Content Freeze
      ↓
Final QA
      ↓
Client Review
      ↓
Acceptance
```

New content introduced after the freeze should follow a controlled change process.

---

# 14. Content Change Process

After launch:

```text
Change Request
      ↓
Scope Review
      ↓
Content Preparation
      ↓
Approval
      ↓
Update
      ↓
QA
      ↓
Publish
```

---

# 15. Content vs Feature Changes

A content change:

```text
Add new product
Update phone number
Replace image
Publish article
```

may fall under maintenance.

A feature change:

```text
Add product comparison engine
Build CMS
Add customer login
Build booking system
```

is additional functionality.

---

# 16. Content Limits

Business Growth provides richer content capability, but not unlimited content production.

The project agreement should specify, where appropriate:

* Initial product count
* Initial service count
* Initial project count
* Initial articles
* Initial resources
* Initial locations
* Initial team members

Large-scale content entry may require additional scope.

* Certifications
* Awards
* Testimonials
* Customer names
* Financial figures
* Project outcomes
* Medical claims
* Educational credentials

```text
 ↓
Human Review
 ↓
Client Verification
 ↓
Approval
 ↓
Publish
```

Every content item must map to an appropriate model.

Example:

```text
Hydraulic Press
    ↓
Product Model
```

```text
Commercial Construction
    ↓
Service Model
```

```text

    ↓

```

Do not create arbitrary structures for individual entries.

# 21. Content Naming Standards

Use consistent names.

Examples:

```text
Hydraulic Press HP-500
Commercial Construction
Apex Tower
Industrial Equipment
Company Profile 2026
```

Avoid:

```text
new product
final product
product latest
image 1
image final 2
```

---

# 22. Slug Management

Public content should use:

```text
lowercase
hyphens
human-readable names
stable identifiers
```

Example:

```text
hydraulic-press
commercial-construction
apex-tower
company-profile
```

Avoid changing established slugs unnecessarily.

# 28. Asset Rights & Licensing

Before publication verify:

```text
[ ] Client owns asset
[ ] Client has usage rights
[ ] Agency has licensed asset
[ ] Stock license allows intended use
[ ] Photographer permission exists where required
[ ] Logo usage is authorized
```

Do not publish assets simply because the client supplied them.

---

# 29. Copyright Responsibility

The client remains responsible for ensuring they have appropriate rights to client-supplied:

* Photos
* Logos
* Videos
* Documents
* Testimonials
* Third-party trademarks

The agency should raise obvious concerns rather than knowingly publishing unauthorized material.

# 30. Image Quality

Images should be:

* Relevant
* Clear
* Correctly cropped
* Appropriately sized
* Optimized
* Consistent with the approved design

Avoid:

* Pixelated images
* Huge originals
* Incorrect aspect ratios
* Unnecessary duplicates

---

# 31. Image Variants

Where practical, create or request:

```text
thumbnail
card
medium
detail
hero
```

rather than using one huge source file everywhere.

---

# 32. Image Dimensions

Image dimensions should match usage.

Example:

```text
Product Card
→ medium image

Hero
→ large image

Thumbnail
→ small image
```

Do not load a 5000px source for a 300px card when unnecessary.

---

# 33. Responsive Images

The application should support responsive image delivery where appropriate.

Possible mechanisms:

```text
srcset
sizes
image CDN transformations
responsive image service
```

The implementation must follow the architecture defined for the project.

---

# 34. Image Formats

Preferred formats may include:

```text

WebP
```

Other formats may remain appropriate for:

* Transparency
* Logos
* Legacy compatibility
* Source preservation

---

# 35. Image Alt Text

Important images should have meaningful alt text.

Examples:

Good:

```text
Hydraulic press installed at manufacturing facility
```

Poor:

```text
image123
```

Decorative images may use empty alt attributes where appropriate.

---

# 36. Alt Text Rules

Alt text should:

* Describe the visual purpose.
* Avoid keyword stuffing.
* Avoid repeating nearby text unnecessarily.
* Reflect the actual image.
* Not invent information.

---

# 37. Image Captions

Captions may contain additional context.

Example:

```text
Apex Tower construction completed in 2024.
```

Use captions where they add actual value.

---

# 38. Image Credits

```text

Photographer:
Source:
License:
```

Do not remove required attribution.

# 39. Image Focal Points

For major responsive images, record a focal point where necessary.

Example:

```text
focalPoint:
  x: 52%
  y: 40%
```

This prevents responsive cropping from cutting out the important subject.

```text

    ↓

```

```text

    ↓
```

# 66. Duplicate Asset Detection

Avoid storing:

```text
project-image.jpg
project-image-1.jpg
project-image-final.jpg
project-image-final2.jpg
```

when they are effectively the same asset.

Maintain one authoritative version.

```text

   ↓
Multiple References
```

rather than:


# 70. Stock Photography

```text

License
Asset ID where applicable

```

---

# 71. AI-Generated Assets

If AI-generated images are used:

* Confirm the client approves them.
* Do not misrepresent generated imagery as actual company photography.
* Do not use fabricated "project" images as evidence of real work.
* Maintain clear distinction between illustrative imagery and factual business imagery.

For example:

A conceptual architectural illustration may be acceptable as an illustration.

A generated image presented as a real completed client project is not acceptable.

# 149. Final Principle

Business Growth depends heavily on structured content.

The package should therefore treat content and assets as a system rather than as files dropped into a website.

The model is:

```text
Collect
   ↓
Organize
   ↓
Verify
   ↓
Approve
   ↓
Structure
   ↓
Optimize
   ↓
Publish
   ↓
Measure
   ↓
Maintain
   ↓
Archive
```

The goal is:

> **Every piece of business information should have a clear model, every important asset should have a clear source of truth, every published item should be approved, and every future update should be possible without rebuilding the website.**

The Business Growth template should therefore make it easy to go from:

```text
New Client
   ↓
New Content
   ↓
New Assets
   ↓
Existing Models
   ↓
Existing Components
   ↓
Existing Templates
   ↓
New Client Website
```

without creating a new content architecture for every client.
